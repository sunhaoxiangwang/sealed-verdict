## Direction contract
seed: cbc8a152341bedf75f5125439ccd0ce6 (direction, experience; assigned index 5 of own grounded list)

THESIS: The interface is the paperwork of a Victorian coroner's inquest held by candlelight: facts are evidence tags, judgments are stamped dockets, the verdict is a sealed writ.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Courtroom: docket strip stamping the live phase, the candle-lit 3D tribunal framed like a window, evidence tags swinging into the ledger at right, brass plates and wax-sealed writs below.
VISITOR PATH: Judges watch the case play, trigger the three writs, then read the certificate as a sealed document and verify it.
SIGNATURE INTERACTION: The ink stamp: phase changes, VERIFIED/FORGED badges and PASS/FAIL rows land with a stamp press.
CROSS-SURFACE REACH: Results and Replay are coroner's report forms; Certificate is the writ. Same paper, ink, brass, typewriter grammar everywhere.
HONEST RISK: Paper-on-dark can read as a theme-park prop if stamps are overused; keep stamps to state changes, keep body type calm.
